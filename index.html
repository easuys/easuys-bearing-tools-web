Calculation on request

EA Suys computes the bearing capacity of strip footings with its own program. The calculation is available on request; it no longer runs on this page.

Send us the footing (width, depth, the load and its inclination), the soil (c′, φ′, unit weights, the water table) and any slope beside the footing. You receive the collapse load and the EN 1997-1 check.

Ask for a calculation

What the calculation covers

  • The exact plasticity solution for a smooth or rough strip footing on c′–φ′ soil with weight, with c′ increasing with depth.
  • A water table at any depth.
  • A slope beside the footing and an inclined load, with moment equilibrium through an effective footing.
  • The Davis reduction for real sand, which dilates less than the exact solution assumes.
  • An EN 1997-1 bearing check with the recommended partial factors, with the Annex D formula beside it.

Checks

  • Prandtl–Reissner closed-form solutions, and Green's exact solution for inclined loads on undrained clay.
  • Published tables (Martin 2004 and 2005, Cox 1962, Salençon and Matar 1982): agreement within 0.03 %.
  • Water table: the exact limits and second-order mesh convergence.
